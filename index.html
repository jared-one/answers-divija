ONE IDEA TO KEEP

What have I proved, and what is still missing?

A good example, a promising diagram or the right numerical result can be useful without being a finished proof. Work from one verified step to the next. You do not need to redo everything at once.

WHAT TO REPAIR FIRSTNOT A NEW EXAM
01

Prove both directions

For “find all” or “if and only if”, show your candidates work and that no others work.

02

Complete a bound

State what cannot happen, then show the boundary can actually be reached.

03

Justify the construction

Name the points, explain why an auxiliary point exists, and state the theorem you are using.

04

Switch strategies deliberately

After a sustained attempt, ask which exact lemma remains and whether your current route can prove it.
