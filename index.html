A small, useful library.

Real BMO problems and how they are marked

Start with UKMT's short marking guide, then try an early question from a recent BMO1 paper. Write out the argument before opening solutions.

Writing understandable mathematical arguments

03

Functional equations: the two obligations

Prove every solution has your proposed form, then prove each candidate works. Start with Section 1; Example 3.1 and the Section 7 checklist are optional.

04

Inequalities: the AM-GM opening

Start with Theorem 1.1, Example 1.2 and optionally Exercises 1.3 to 1.4. Stop before Muirhead. Use the Proof Laboratory for the Schur example.

What counts as a proof?

A relaxed essay by Evan Chen. Sections 1, 3 and 5 are useful; the formal foundations in Section 4 are optional and unnecessary for this preparation.
