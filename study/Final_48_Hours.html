START HERE / 16–18 NOVEMBER 2026

Two tracks, one decision

This page gives an immediate two-day route before the scheduled BMO1 and a long-term route that remains available afterward. They are alternatives, not a checklist that must all be completed.

EXAM PRIORITY / 20–75 min

One short proof repair, one genuinely unseen exercise, then a clean final-writeup. Rest is part of the plan.

DEEP LIBRARY / SELF-PACED

Read full geometry, functional equations, number theory, algebra or combinatorics notes; open international papers when ready.

Since the examination is on 18 November 2026, we do not prescribe a marathon or claim any score target is safe. Confirm the sitting time and entry with school.

MONDAY 16 NOV / OPTIONAL

Recover one proof, not eight

  1. Choose one repair (15–25 minutes): an unfinished combinatorics argument, inequality equality classification or a geometry circle lemma. Close the solution before writing.
  2. Take one small new test (20–35 minutes): select the exam matching today's weakest skill. It is legitimate to stop when concentration drops.
  3. Write one final proof cleanly (10–15 minutes). Compare with the model only afterward.
  4. Wind down and sleep. Losing sleep for a larger unchecked resource list is likely to reduce performance.
Optional specific geometry questionIf you want to try exactly one authentic geometry task today, open MathsDB BMO1 2021 Q2 (verified topic: Geometry). Use a bounded 20–30-minute attempt, write what you can justify, and stop if you need rest. This is one choice, not an additional assignment. Read the practice guide.
TUESDAY 17 NOV / OPTIONAL

Proof quality and a calm final review

Begin with the principle “one complete solution is more valuable than several unproved ideas”. Select a fresh early-BMO problem from a verified paper; work for a bounded block; review a theorem only if it addresses a genuine gap.

Optional 30-minute mixed paper: one algebra/number-theory proof and one combinatorics or geometry proof. If tired, review the proof-audit checklist and stop. There is no requirement to use all available content.

WEDNESDAY 18 NOV / EXAMINATION

On the day

The official BMO1 has six proof-based questions in 3½ hours. Read all problems briefly; select the one with the clearest route to a complete proof. Rough work is where you explore; the final script is where you justify.

Keep track of time, but do not panic if a question is unfamiliar. An early proof well written can outperform six partial starts. Check UKMT rules and school instructions, including the prohibition on calculators and the confidentiality of current contest questions.

Official current BMO1 instructions.

BEYOND BMO1 / NO CEILING

Keep learning at full depth

After the sitting, the library opens into complete subject notes, past BMO1 and BMO2 archives, APMO, EGMO, CMO and IMO collections. You can choose by topic, difficulty and purpose instead of following a fixed progression.

Doing a problem slowly is not wasted if you can explain its key insight, reproduce the proof and transfer it. A short proof skill that is mastered often beats a large amount of passive reading.

ASSESSMENT HONESTY

What marks do and do not tell you

The recorded 21/56 from the original Benchmark 3 photo review and 28/60 from the historical 2019 BMO practice review describe particular scripts in particular conditions, and are not comparable official exam scores. An earlier self-reported 8/60 requires the paper, sitting date and health/context to be confirmed before interpreting any apparent improvement.

Previous claims about a “safe” BMO2 cutoff should not be used. Qualification is determined by the current rules and year's invitations; the 2027 BMO2 will be the invitation-only National Final. Focus on the next proof rather than treating a speculative cutoff as a promise.
