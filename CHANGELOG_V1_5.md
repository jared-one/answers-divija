## Evidence / QA caveats
- Marks are provisional educational judgments, not official UKMT results.
- Archive data and topic tags are attributed to MathsDB and may change. Original contest problems remain with their publisher and are not copied into this project.
- Links, SHA-256, ZIP integrity, JavaScript syntax and printable rendering were checked. Local Chromium screenshot attempt did not complete. Test actual browser interaction after deployment.
- Existing legacy learning pages without a main landmark retain their original layout.
